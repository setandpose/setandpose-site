Privacy

Effective date: November 7, 2026.

Set & Pose analyzes gym videos on your iPhone to suggest posing photos and training clips. The app needs no account. Your first 3 videos in each mode are free; after that, saving or sharing new results needs the Set & Pose Pro subscription, bought and billed through Apple. Set & Pose never sees your payment details; it keeps only Apple's signed record that a subscription is active and a count of free videos used, both on your iPhone. Set & Pose uploads nothing: it has no automatic media upload, advertising tracker or analytics SDK.

Your video and photos

Imports use Apple's Photos or Files picker. When you save, Set & Pose asks for Photos access so it can add your photos and clips and show them again in its Library, by pose and by date. Limit Access works; Add Photos Only still saves, but the Library cannot show those photos until you select them. To find what it saved, the app keeps a small index on your iPhone: the date, mode, division, pose, side, format and the Photos identifier of each saved item, never the pixels. Clear library in Settings removes that index; it never deletes anything from Photos. Set & Pose does not request camera, microphone or location access and does not modify your original imported video.

Working files stay in protected local storage excluded from device backups. Body and face landmarks used during analysis are temporary and are not kept in reports. Set & Pose does not use face recognition to identify people.

A finished posing review may be retained locally for up to a week so you can continue it, including pose labels, kept photos and saved status. Done, Discard or starting a new session removes that review; expired reviews are removed when the app next performs cleanup. Other unfinished working sessions are cleaned up on relaunch. Settings remain on your device until changed or the app is deleted.

Copies you explicitly save to Photos or share are separate from temporary app files. Removing a review or deleting Set & Pose does not delete those copies. Manage them in Photos or the destination you chose. Those destinations may have their own backup, sync and privacy settings.

Optional footage you send us

The app uploads nothing. Some beta testers may choose to send a posing video to us directly, by AirDrop or iMessage. That is never required. A video sent this way is used only to check and improve pose suggestions, is not posted or shared, and is deleted on request.

Feedback and TestFlight

When you contact support, your email and the details you choose to include are received in the Set & Pose Gmail inbox. The inbox is accessed only by the Set & Pose owner. Support messages are kept while an issue is unresolved and deleted within 12 months after it is resolved. Please do not attach photos or videos; describe the issue instead.

Apple separately processes TestFlight beta diagnostics and feedback under its TestFlight privacy information. This policy does not describe TestFlight as collecting no data.
